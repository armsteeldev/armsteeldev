## Overview

The Echo Archives is an independent, curated discovery platform for audio dramas and fiction podcasts. It helps listeners decide what to listen to next and gives each show a considered place in the archive.

## Discovery and context

The site brings together a searchable catalog, show pages, curated collections, and recommendation paths. The aim is to make the next listening choice easier while keeping information about each show clear and useful.
