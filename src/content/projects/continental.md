## Overview

Continental brings together a broader body of software, services, and technical work under one identity. The projects can stand on their own while forming part of a connected ecosystem.

## One part of the ecosystem

Continental Cloud is a private, self-hosted drive and sync service within that work. It keeps file contents on disk and uses SQLite for searchable metadata, so the underlying files remain accessible through the filesystem itself.
