## Overview

MIDAS is a prediction-market research command center with a deterministic virtual exchange. It examines complementary binary positions using observed order-book depth, then models risk decisions and sequential execution in a simulated venue.

## A clear simulation boundary

The platform can observe public market information and run offline synthetic scenarios, but it never submits real orders or handles wallets or deposited funds. Its simulations are research outputs, not evidence of real fills or profitability.
